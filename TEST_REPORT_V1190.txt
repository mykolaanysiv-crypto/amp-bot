ROOT-CAUSE FIX FOUND DURING FINAL QA
The first draft of migration 0016 represented `credential_id_b64` as a UniqueConstraint plus a separate non-unique index, while SQLAlchemy `mapped_column(unique=True, index=True)` represents it as one UNIQUE index. This would risk a schema-drift gate failure. The migration was corrected to create `ix_web_authn_credentials_credential_id_b64` with `unique=True`, matching canonical model metadata. Tests were rerun after the fix.
