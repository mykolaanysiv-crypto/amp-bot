9. WAITLIST / ATTENDANCE
------------------------
Waitlist capacity occupancy: registered/reserved/checked_in/attended.
Waitlist promotion: FIFO by waitlisted_at.
Reservation duration: 2 hours.
Reservation notification: durable Telegram outbox with confirm callback.
Expired reservation: returns to waitlisted and next participant can be promoted.
Cancellation processes the queue immediately.
Completed event registered/reserved participants are marked no_show.

Human labels:
- registered = Зареєстрований
- cancelled = Скасував
- waitlisted = У черзі
- reserved = Місце зарезервовано
- checked_in = Відмічено присутність
- attended = Був присутній
- no_show = Не прийшов
