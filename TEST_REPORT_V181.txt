6. IMPORTANT v1.8.1 SAFETY PROPERTIES
--------------------------------------
- `deleted` and `deleted_permanent` are automatic lifecycle statuses; normal
  status edit/approval routes cannot set or manually clear them.
- Soft deletion preserves participant data, XP, participation history and
  reporting relations.
- `deleted_permanent` has no restoration route.
- Superadmin restoration starts a 14-day probation.
- Probation is completed automatically after qualifying recorded participation;
  otherwise status becomes deleted_permanent after expiry.
- New-content notifications use existing durable broadcast/outbox reliability.
- v1.7.3 security and v1.7.4 reliability mechanisms were not removed.
