8. REFERRAL CLAWBACK
- Only `rewarded` referral can be revoked.
- Window: <=30 days from referral reward.
- Exact stored referral XP is reversed using `referral_reversal` transaction.
- Referral becomes `revoked` and stores reason/time/clawback amount.
- Durable notification queued to inviter.
- TelegramForbiddenError in outbox/broadcast marks active participant/ambassador inactive and invokes the same clawback rule.
